# InvoiceApp - Professional Invoice & Proposal Management System

## Overview

InvoiceApp is a comprehensive, web-based invoice and proposal management system designed specifically for contractors, construction businesses, and service providers. Built with modern web technologies, it streamlines the billing process, tracks payments, manages clients, and provides powerful tools for business operations.

## Key Features

### Invoice Management
- **Create Professional Invoices**: Generate detailed invoices with customizable templates
- **Multiple Order Types**: Support for Day Work, Contract, and Extra work orders
- **Payment Tracking**: Monitor payment status, pending balances, and payment history
- **Multi-Currency Support**: Handle invoices in different currencies with exchange rates
- **Tax Management**: Flexible tax rate configuration per company
- **PDF Generation**: High-quality PDF exports optimized for printing and sharing

### Proposal System
- **Professional Proposals**: Create detailed proposals with work descriptions and payment terms
- **Conversion to Invoice**: Seamlessly convert approved proposals to invoices
- **Job Information**: Include job name, location, and detailed work descriptions
- **Payment Terms**: Specify payment schedules and terms clearly
- **Digital Signatures**: Collect client signatures electronically
- **Validity Tracking**: Set and monitor proposal expiration dates
- **Industry Templates**: Pre-built templates for Contractors, Freelancers, Agencies, and Consultants
- **Activity Timeline**: Visual lifecycle tracking (Created → Sent → Viewed → Signed → Paid)

### Quick Setup Wizard
- **2-Minute Onboarding**: Get started in under 2 minutes with guided setup
- **Step-by-Step Process**: 6 easy steps to configure your business
- **Company Profile**: Set up your business name and details
- **Logo Upload**: Add your company branding instantly
- **Currency Selection**: Choose from 15+ currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, PEN, etc.)
- **Tax Configuration**: Set your default tax rate and name
- **Progress Tracking**: Visual progress indicator throughout setup

### Client Management
- **Client Database**: Comprehensive client information management
- **Document Management**: Store client documents (RUC, DNI, etc.)
- **Contact Information**: Track emails, phones, and addresses
- **Client History**: View complete billing and payment history per client

### Job Tracking
- **Work Logs**: Track hours worked, workers, and job progress
- **Photo Documentation**: Upload and organize job site photos by date
- **Receipt Management**: Attach expense receipts to jobs and invoices
- **Progress Monitoring**: Track job completion and milestones

### Financial Features
- **Payment Processing**: Record payments with multiple payment methods
- **Payment History**: Comprehensive payment tracking and reporting
- **Outstanding Balances**: Monitor accounts receivable
- **Financial Reports**: Generate reports on income, pending payments, and more
- **Multi-Company Support**: Manage multiple business entities

### Digital Signatures
- **Electronic Signatures**: Collect client signatures on invoices
- **Email Integration**: Send signature requests via email
- **Signature Tracking**: Monitor signature status (pending, signed, expired)
- **Legal Compliance**: Timestamped and secure signature collection
- **Company Signatures**: Add company signatures to proposals and invoices

### Customization
- **Company Branding**: Upload company logo and customize branding
- **Multi-Language**: English and Spanish language support
- **Dark Mode**: User preference for light/dark themes
- **Custom Tax Rates**: Configure company-specific tax rates
- **Series Configuration**: Customizable invoice and proposal numbering

### User Experience
- **Responsive Design**: Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **Intuitive Interface**: Clean, modern UI with easy navigation
- **Real-time Updates**: Instant feedback on actions
- **Search & Filters**: Powerful search and filtering capabilities
- **Bulk Operations**: Perform actions on multiple records
- **Visual Section Icons**: Form sections with intuitive icons for better navigation
- **Animated Interactions**: Smooth transitions and feedback with Framer Motion
- **Activity Timelines**: Track document lifecycle at a glance

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: Next.js 14, React, TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM
- **Storage**: Supabase Storage
- **PDF Generation**: html2canvas, jsPDF
- **Authentication**: JWT-based authentication
- **Internationalization**: next-intl
